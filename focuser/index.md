# Focuser — Do what matters first

Focuser is a free iPhone app that puts one small condition in front of the apps that usually take your time: write 1 to 3 focus tasks for the day, confirm the list, and finish them. Until you do, the apps you chose stay restricted. When the list is done, they open until midnight Thailand time.

- **Privacy policy:** [read it here](privacy)
- **Support:** email **purichseenuallae@gmail.com**. Replies come in Thai or English, usually within a few days.
- **Price:** free. No in-app purchases, no subscription, no renewals.
- **Requires:** iPhone, iOS 17 or later.

## How a day works

1. Write 1 to 3 focus tasks in your own words.
2. Confirm the list. It stays fixed for the rest of the day.
3. Mark each task done as you finish it.
4. When every task is done, your selected apps open until midnight Thailand time.

A new day starts at 00:00 in Bangkok, even when you travel. Anything unfinished carries into the next day as a draft for you to review and confirm again.

## Common questions

**Which apps get restricted?** Only the ones you pick yourself, using Apple's own Screen Time picker. Leave your study apps and the people you need to reach out of the selection.

**What if something urgent comes up?** Once per day you can write a reason and take 10 minutes of access. Those are ten elapsed minutes, and they do not count as finishing your list.

**Does Focuser check that I really did the task?** No. Completion is self-reported: the app records that you tapped done. If you tap by mistake, you can take the mark back the same day.

**Is this parental control?** No. Focuser is a voluntary tool for your own device. It does not stop you from changing Screen Time settings or deleting the app.

**Where is my data?** On your iPhone only. There is no account, no cloud, no ads, and no analytics. See the [privacy policy](privacy).
